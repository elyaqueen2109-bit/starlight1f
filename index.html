📝 Grammar

The -ing form

  • в роли подлежащего: Drinking milk is good for you.
  • после глаголов: admit, appreciate, avoid, consider, continue, deny, fancy, go, imagine, mind, miss, quit, save, suggest, practise, prevent: I suggest eating out tonight.
  • после love, like, enjoy, prefer, dislike, hate — общее предпочтение: I love watching old films.
    НО: с would like / would prefer / would love — инфинитив с to.
  • после be busy, it's no use, it's (not) worth, there's no point (in), can't help, can't stand, have difficulty (in), look forward to, object to, prefer (doing to doing): I look forward to seeing you.
  • после spend, waste, lose (time, money): Don't waste time trying.
  • после предлогов: John is good at fixing computers.

Infinitive with to

  • для цели: He's saving money to buy a car.
  • после agree, appear, decide, expect, hope, plan, promise, refuse: I promise to return the money.
  • после would like / would prefer / would love — определённое предпочтение: I would love to go to the theatre.
  • после прилагательных чувств (happy, glad, sad), желания (eager, reluctant, willing), характера (clever, kind), а также lucky, fortunate: He's always eager to help out.
  • после too / enough: It is too cold to go swimming.
  • после to tell you the truth, to be honest, to sum up, to begin with: To tell you the truth, I didn't think they'd win.

Infinitive without to

  • после модальных глаголов: He should see a doctor.
  • после let, make, see, hear, feel: They made him pay extra.
    НО: в пассиве — с to: He was made to pay extra.
  • после would rather (not) / had better (not): You had better book early.
  • после help — можно и с to, и без: I helped her (to) move the sofa.

Различия в значении

forget + to-inf = not remember — He forgot to take his wallet.
forget + -ing = not recall — I'll never forget skiing down the Alps.
remember + to-inf = not forget — Did you remember to wish Sue a happy birthday?
remember + -ing = recall — I remember going bungee jumping.
mean + to-inf = intend to — I didn't mean to hurt her feelings.
mean + -ing = involve — It will mean spending a lot on petrol.
regret + to-inf = be sorry to — I regret to inform you...
regret + -ing = feel sorry about — I regret spending so much money.
try + to-inf = attempt — I tried to open the drawer.
try + -ing = as an experiment — He should try cutting down on fatty foods.
stop + to-inf = stop in order to do sth — We stopped to take photos.
stop + -ing = finish doing — He stopped talking when the teacher walked in.

Voluntourism: how to make a difference!

More and more young people are taking on volunteer work nowadays. Some even travel to the other side of the world and do unpaid work for a charity or other non-profit organisation there. They help to build homes for poor families, teach children English, take care of orphans, or help to protect an endangered species. There is a wide variety of voluntary jobs to choose from.

A number of charity groups organise educational programmes for children around the world. Many students volunteer for these types of projects. They teach subjects like English and Mathematics to children while staying in their community and getting to know the people and their culture. It is also a great opportunity for them to work and train with local teachers and develop their own skills. It is a learning experience for both the children and the teacher.

Young people who are good with their hands or simply would like to help in a very practical way can participate in one of the many construction projects. Several charity organisations help build houses or schools for poor children around the world. In the village of Kamakwie in Sierra Leone, for example, a team of young volunteers built a secondary school for poor orphans.

For people interested in wildlife, conservation groups organise volunteer holidays throughout the world. You can help wildlife research in countries like Kenya and Botswana or protect turtles in Mexico, Zanzibar, or elsewhere. On Redang Island in Malaysia volunteers come from all over the world to help conserve the endangered green turtle. Participants monitor the turtles that come onto the beach and protect them and their eggs. They also get to enjoy the beach and even snorkel with the turtles sometimes.

A volunteer holiday is for people who want to make a difference in the world and are prepared to give up their own time, money, and effort to do it. Volunteers have to be realistic though and understand that they cannot change the world in two or three weeks. To some volunteers it seems that they are not doing much by helping out for only a couple of weeks but, in fact, every bit of help counts. Thanks to young volunteers, the lives of millions of people around the world are improving and, in their eyes, the sun is probably shining brighter than it used to.
